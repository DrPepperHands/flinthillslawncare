What we do

One crew for the whole yard, all season long.

From weekly mowing to irrigation and full landscape beds, we handle it so you don't have to juggle three companies. Serving residential, commercial, and HOA properties.

Lawn Mowing & Maintenance

Reliable weekly mowing, trimming, and edging. We pick up and haul off your clippings — most crews just mulch and leave.

Fertilization & Lawn Health

Fertilization, weed & insect control, aeration and overseeding — the treatments that turn a nice lawn into an amazing one.

Irrigation & Sprinklers

Start-ups, backflow testing, repairs, and fall winterization. New system installs available through Dixon Irrigation. We do it right and on time.

Seasonal Cleanups

Spring cleanups, fall leaf removal, bed refreshes, and gutter cleaning. All leaves and debris hauled away.

Landscaping & Lighting

Our story

Started by a Wichita firefighter. Still family-run.

Flint Hills began in 2018, when a local firefighter picked up professional equipment to care for his own lawn. Neighbors started asking him to take care of theirs — and the rest is history.

Today we're a father-and-son operation that employs other local first responders. We've spent years educating ourselves and our team to bring you the best family-owned lawn service in the Wichita area. Give us a call and let us help make your neighbors jealous.
